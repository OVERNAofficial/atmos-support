MET NORWAY
Weather forecast data: Norwegian Meteorological Institute (MET Norway),
Locationforecast, licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International.
https://api.met.no/doc/License
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
https://api.met.no/weatherapi/locationforecast/2.0/documentation
Atmos converts units and documented weather symbols, groups timestamps by local
calendar day and computes extrema of available samples. These are adaptations,
not official MET daily extrema. Solar times are calculated locally with SunCalc.
Forecast periods remain one or six hours; no hourly interpolation is invented.
Roughly nine days replace the previous sixteen. Missing probability, gusts,
visibility, rain/snow partition and all-sky UV are not synthesized. Clear-sky UV
is not substituted for all-sky UV. Partial days lack daily precipitation totals.
The service's Expires / Last-Modified headers control caching and conditional
requests. Applications exceeding 20 aggregate requests/second need an agreement;
higher traffic may require a caching proxy. This app includes no paid proxy.
https://api.met.no/doc/TermsOfService

GEONAMES
City and time-zone metadata: GeoNames, CC BY 4.0. See the bundled geonames.txt
notice for the processed snapshot, attribution, adaptations and input checksums.

NASA POWER
The data were obtained from the NASA Langley Research Center POWER Project
funded through the NASA Earth Science Directorate Applied Science Program.
https://power.larc.nasa.gov/docs/referencing/
https://power.larc.nasa.gov/docs/methodology/data/sources/
Daily meteorology uses MERRA-2 / GEOS-IT at 0.5° latitude × 0.625° longitude.
Atmos requests UTC days and converts wind units. Coverage starts in 1981,
replacing the previous 1940 ERA5 start. Gridded values are not local station
measurements. Recent publication lags; historic values can be revised. The data
record can span different models, which matters for climate-trend analysis.

NOAA / NATIONAL WEATHER SERVICE
https://www.weather.gov/disclaimer
The NOAA/NWS material identified here is public-domain government information,
not subject to Atmos copyright. Atmos-rendered maps, labels and unit conversions
must not be understood as unaltered official government products. NWS source
bulletins and CPC graphics retain their issuing identity and dates.
Products: US alerts, observations, forecasts and discussions; CONUS MRMS radar;
NEXRAD radial velocity; NDFD weather fields; GOES Americas infrared; NHC tropical
tracks, cones, wind footprints and advisories; CPC extended outlooks.
NOAA-derived lightning density is distributed by nowCOAST as a Level 5 product
appropriate for public distribution. It incorporates NLDN / GLD360 inputs; the
underlying commercial network feed is not provided by Atmos.
https://nowcoast.noaa.gov/geoserver/observations/lightning_detection/ows?service=WMS&request=GetCapabilities
Tropical model tracks are limited to NOAA GFS / GEFS and selected NOAA hurricane
model aids identified in NHC's techlist. Foreign and multi-center consensus
models are excluded. A model track is not the official NHC forecast.
https://ftp.nhc.noaa.gov/atcf/docs/nhc_techlist.dat

IOWA ENVIRONMENTAL MESONET
NOAA HRRR regional simulated reflectivity, and published storm/rotation feeds.
https://mesonet.agron.iastate.edu/disclaimer.php
IEM data are available for lawful use under its public-domain policy.
The 18-hour HRRR timeline uses the published completed-run marker and 15-minute
steps. Atmos renders provider imagery; it does not create forecast values or
extend model coverage. Rotation markers are not tornado confirmations.

LIMITS OF THIS VERSION
No global modeled AQI, numeric 30-day ensemble/quantile export, correlation
coefficient radar, global wind particles, global infrared mosaic, GFS future-radar
extension, or cameras outside the selected California districts. Observed radar
is CONUS. Recent 48-hour history uses nearby US station reports, not a global
model archive; reports may be incomplete and prior-hour rainfall values overlap.
CPC extended graphics remain, but are not substitutes for numeric ensembles.

All sources can be delayed or unavailable. Check issue times and coverage.
Use official authorities for emergency instructions. Atmos does not provide
background emergency notification delivery. Data providers and software authors
do not endorse Atmos. Provider terms can change; no unlimited-capacity or
perpetual availability guarantee is made.

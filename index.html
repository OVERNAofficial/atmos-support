Atmos weather

Weather forecasts, radar, official advisories and historical context in one workspace. Published by OVERNA Technologies.

Contact support

Email jordan@overna.io. Include your app version, device, selected city and the screen involved. Do not send passwords or sensitive personal information.

Using Atmos

  • Choose a city with the location search, or use optional foreground device location.
  • Open the menu to explore forecasts, radar, advisories, history, briefing and settings.
  • Use Settings to change units and review privacy, data sources and licenses.
  • Weather sources need an internet connection. City search and bundled documentation work offline.

Coverage in version 0.4.0

MET Norway provides approximately nine forecast days. Later forecast periods have six-hour spacing. Observed radar focuses on the continental United States. NASA POWER history starts in 1981 and uses UTC days. Recent station observations are US-focused.

This version does not include global AQI, a numeric 30-day ensemble, global wind particles or most camera networks. Cameras are limited to selected Caltrans still images. See the complete source and coverage notes.

Location or loading problems

If you deny location access, search for a city instead. To change access later, use your device’s location-permission settings. If a weather source is unavailable, check your connection and retry later; a missing reading is not zero. Check timestamps when viewing forecasts, radar or camera imagery.

Safety

Atmos does not deliver background emergency alerts. Follow official authorities for emergency instructions.

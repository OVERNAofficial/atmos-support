Atmos privacy policy

Effective October 9, 2026 · OVERNA Technologies

What stays on your device

The installed Android and Apple apps store selected and saved places, measurement units, display preferences and camera favorites locally. There is no Atmos account, advertising SDK, analytics SDK or Atmos-operated weather server in this build. City search uses a bundled GeoNames index; typed city searches are not sent to a search provider.

Location and network requests

Device location is optional and requested while you use the app. You can choose a city instead and revoke location permission in system settings. The operating system’s location and place-name services may process coordinates under Apple’s or your Android service provider’s policies.

Weather requests send the selected coordinates to the relevant data provider. Map providers receive requested map areas; camera providers receive requests for the cameras you open. Providers also receive your IP address, request time and app identification. These requests go directly from the installed app to the providers listed in Settings & sources. Their privacy and retention policies apply. No continuous background location collection or background emergency-alert delivery is implemented.

Retention and deletion

Preferences remain until you change them or clear the app’s storage. Remove individual saved places here or remove camera favorites in Cameras. To clear all local app data, use Android’s Clear storage option or delete the app on iPhone or iPad; offloading an Apple app retains its documents and data. Device or system backups may follow your system settings. Temporary weather caches are used to reduce repeat network requests. Provider access logs are controlled by those providers; OVERNA Technologies cannot delete them from your device.

Exports and contact

Exported files and items you share are controlled by the destination you choose. If you email support, your email address and the information you send are processed to answer your request; please avoid sending unnecessary sensitive information. Contact jordan@overna.io for support-data deletion requests.

Changes

This policy describes the installed app in this build. Material changes to data handling will be reflected in an updated policy and applicable store disclosures. The website, if deployed, also involves its hosting provider.
